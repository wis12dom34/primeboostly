GOOD QUESTIONS. CLEAR ANSWERS.

Before you get started.

What is PrimeBoostly?+

A social media growth platform offering followers, likes, views and other services across multiple social networks.

How do I place an order?+

Create an account, fund your wallet, choose a service, then enter your link and quantity in the existing dashboard.

How quickly do orders start?+

Start times and completion times vary by service. Check the service details before ordering.

Which platforms are supported?+

The public landing page lists Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, X / Twitter, Telegram and Spotify. Check the service catalogue for current availability.

How do I fund my wallet?+

Sign in and use Add Funds to see the payment options currently available to your account.

Can I track my orders?+

Use the Orders section of your dashboard to follow order progress.

Do you support resellers and provide an API?+

Yes. The existing API documentation covers services, placing orders, single and multiple order status, refills and account balance.

Where can I contact support?+

Visit the existing Contact page using the Support link below.
